Built for Plots, Flats & Villas

Whether you are marketing plotted land developments, high-rise apartment towers, or gated villa communities, EstateMark delivers real-time visibility, automated status synchronization, and zero double-booking assurance.

Plotted Land

Plotted Layouts & Land Projects

Comprehensive spatial visualization and inventory management engineered specifically for land promoters and plotted township developments.

  • Interactive Master Layouts: Explore the original layout of the property to easily understand plot locations, available plots, and the overall arrangement.
  • Color-Coded Status Tracking: Instant visibility into Available (Green), On Hold (Amber), Booked (Blue), and Blocked (Purple) plots across the entire layout.
  • Orientation & Dimension Clarity: Review plot dimensions, total square yards, cardinal facings (East, West, North, South), and internal road widths (30', 40', 60').
  • Plot Availability Overview: View plot details and availability information in an organized layout to help customers identify suitable plots.
Zero Double-Booking Guarantee
Gated Enclaves

Villas & Luxury Communities

High-touch inventory management and construction progress alignment for premium gated villa projects, row houses, and luxury enclaves.

  • Phase-Wise Villa Mapping: Manage individual villa plots, distinct elevation designs, and phased community releases with complete status transparency.
  • Milestone & Stage Tracking: Track booking milestones, foundation progress, structure completion, and customer handover stages alongside inventory states.
  • Elevated Client Presentation: Equip senior relationship managers with sleek mobile and tablet presentations showcasing exact orientation, clubhouses, and green zones.
  • Property Details at a Glance: Review key details about available properties, including plot information and relevant layout details, in one convenient place.

  • Sales Gallery & HQ Synchronization: Keep apartment availability and sales information organized so teams can easily check the latest inventory details.
