At Estatemark, developed by DeccanIDentity Software Private Limited, we envision a future where property inventory management is transparent, synchronized, and truly empowering. We build the digital bridge between developers and their sales networks — transforming how plots, flats, and villas are visualized, reserved, and managed in real time.

Estatemark simplifies every step of the sales lifecycle — from instant availability lookup to secure booking reservations across Android, iOS, and desktop web.

Our commitment: Make every property smarter, every booking safer, and every decision clearer with an enterprise 99.9% uptime SLA.
